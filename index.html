How it works

From undiscovered talent to scouted athlete, in four steps:

1

Showcase

Student creates a profile with position, achievements and where they train.

2

Get scouted

Scouts search the Talent Directory, shortlist students and invite them to trials.

3

Train nearby

Find the nearest court, pitch or track and keep improving while you wait.

4

Show up & shine

Student attends the trial, performs, and the scout gives feedback through the platform.

🎤 Our hackathon pitch (30 seconds)

Problem: Talented children and students across Kenya are never discovered — scouts only see players at big tournaments or famous schools, so ability without connections goes to waste. And many young athletes don't even know where they can train nearby.
Solution: TalantaLink — one website where students showcase their talent to verified scouts, scouts search and shortlist the next generation, and everyone finds their nearest training facility.
